## Selection standard

Every candidate should have:
1. A recognizable actual problem in food, travelling, multiplayer/IRL interaction, or optimization.
2. A new user action or a substantive improvement over identified prior art.
3. A technical core that matters to the outcome, not ornamental AI or gratuitous agents.
4. A distinctive interaction model: direct manipulation, shared physical interaction, spatial comparison, or another task-specific interface. A stylish landing page is insufficient.
5. A judge-controlled demonstration under three minutes showing a real state change.
6. An ambitious product vision and a coherent overnight implementation that proves its central claim.

Prior-art existence is not an automatic rejection: the rubric explicitly permits a fresh approach. Conversely, absence from our search is not proof of novelty. Distinguish direct clones, meaningful workflow innovations, and untested research hypotheses.
