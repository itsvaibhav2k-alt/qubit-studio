# Deeper interaction research: boundaries and enabling precedents

Sources inspected live during the track-first pass; these are not new candidate endorsements.

## Mappedin

https://www.mappedin.com/

Official page describes AI-assisted floor-plan conversion, editable interactive indoor maps, wayfinding, venue operations and SDKs. Therefore "photograph a map/floor plan and make it interactive" is not enough differentiation by itself. A travel concept needs to solve an additional specific failure or user task.

## SuperCook

https://www.supercook.com/

Public interface exposes a detailed pantry ingredient selector and voice entry. Its onboarding explicitly says each ingredient unlocks more recipes. Therefore "tell us what's in your fridge and get recipes" is established functionality, not a useful novelty claim. No authenticated use or recommendation-quality testing performed.

## Potluck, Ink & Switch

https://www.inkandswitch.com/potluck/

Research prototype, October 2022. Freeform notes are gradually enriched using searches that extract data, live computations, and dynamic annotations in the original document. Examples include recipe scaling, bill splitting, trip planning and chores. The authors explicitly situate this in prior document-as-interface work and note programmer-oriented testing.

Transferable principle: computation need not be hidden behind chat or a separate dashboard. It can change the user's existing artifact directly. Do not copy recipe scaling or call the overall dynamic-document pattern new.

## Explorable Explanations, Bret Victor

https://worrydream.com/ExplorableExplanations/

Essay and interactive examples first published in 2011, with a 2024 postscript. Explorable arguments expose assumptions and computational models so a reader can change them and challenge conclusions. The author distinguishes this from merely adding interactive pictures.

Transferable principle for the design award: make the important consequence manipulable and visible, not decorative. For an optimization project, show how an intervention changes a computed outcome and why. A graph or slider is not evidence unless backed by a real, stated model.

## Selection implication

A novel interaction can use established technology. The contribution must be the specific new capability or problem resolution, with an explicit nearest analogue. Do not reject every idea merely because its components existed; do not call a familiar whole product new because it is assembled with a different model.

A third site, https://www.bringtogether.com/en/, refused connection; no conclusions about its product are drawn.
